About this project

Smart Stocktopus is a stock research app that pairs live market data with an AI price forecast, and is upfront about how much (and how little) that forecast can tell you.

Why an octopus?

Octopuses are some of the smartest animals in the sea. They solve puzzles, open jars and use tools, and about two-thirds of their neurons sit in their arms, so each arm can explore on its own while one brain ties it all together.

Smart Stocktopus works the same way: eight arms reaching into the market (live prices, AI forecasts, Monte Carlo ranges, a technical scorecard, strategy backtests, news sentiment, comparisons and your portfolio) and one brain making sense of it.

The AI model

A two-layer LSTM reads the last 60 trading days of price, volume, moving averages, MACD and RSI, normalised to the latest close, and predicts the next day's return. Multi-week forecasts roll that forward one day at a time.

Daily prices are mostly noise, so a hit rate a little above 50% is typical. That's why every forecast also shows a Monte Carlo range and the model's live track record.

How it's built

1Data

Prices, quotes, search and headlines come from Yahoo Finance's public endpoints, cached in memory and at Vercel's CDN.

2Training

TensorFlow trains the LSTM offline on 10 years of daily data, with a chronological train/validation/test split.

3Export

The weights are exported to a 120 KB NumPy file. A pure NumPy forward pass matches Keras to within 1e-7.

4Serving

A Flask app on Vercel serverless functions. No TensorFlow at runtime keeps the bundle around 125 MB instead of over 1 GB.
